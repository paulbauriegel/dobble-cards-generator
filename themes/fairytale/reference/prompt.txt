CINDERELLA MATCHING-CARD ICON PROMPT

Create a square matching-game symbol representing the classic Cinderella fairy
tale: one elegant transparent glass slipper with a tiny magical sparkle. Render
it as a bold, polished storybook icon with a thick clean outline, simple rounded
forms, high contrast, and a strong compact silhouette. Center the entire symbol
with generous transparent padding. It must remain instantly recognizable when
printed only 25 mm wide and when the circular card is viewed at any rotation.

Use an original public-domain fairy-tale interpretation. Use icy blue glass,
white highlights, a dark navy outline, and one warm golden sparkle.

Constraints: genuinely transparent background; exactly one slipper; no person,
second shoe, scenery, floor, frame, circle, badge, text, letters, logo, watermark,
cast shadow, or tiny decorative details.

STYLE VARIATIONS USED

1. Flat storybook
   Polished flat storybook illustration, bold navy outline, simple rounded
   shapes, subtle highlights, pale blue glass, warm gold sparkle.

2. Layered paper-cut
   Layered paper-cut illustration, crisp scalloped shapes, thick plum outline,
   pale lavender and silver facets, three tiny star-shaped sparkles.

3. Painted game token
   Richly colored hand-painted board-game token icon, smooth gouache texture,
   bold cream outer keyline, icy blue facets, one pale-gold sparkle.
