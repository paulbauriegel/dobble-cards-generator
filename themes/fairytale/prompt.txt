Copy/paste prompt
-----------------

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: individual symbol for a family-friendly circular visual-matching
card game

Input image: If Image 1 is supplied, use it only as the style, rendering,
outline, composition, and quality reference. Do not reproduce its slipper,
bow, colors, or Cinderella-specific content in the new symbol.

Primary request: Create one original fairy-tale icon representing [CONCEPT].
Show exactly one clear main subject with these unmistakable identifying
features: [2 OR 3 DISTINCTIVE FEATURES]. Present it in this simple view or
pose: [SIMPLE VIEW OR POSE].

Style and rendering: Match the approved reference's polished flat storybook
look. Use smooth, rounded, vector-like shapes; a very thick solid pure-black
outer contour; thinner pure-black interior lines; crisp faceted cel shading;
large clean white highlights; and only a few broad internal color shapes.
Keep the finish bright, friendly, playful, and premium. It should look like a
professionally illustrated family board-game token, not like clip art,
a photograph, a 3D render, or a detailed book scene.

Color palette: Use [2 OR 3 CONCEPT-APPROPRIATE COLORS], supported by white
highlights and the pure-black outlines. Use strong contrast between adjacent
areas. An optional small golden magical sparkle may be placed close to the
subject only when it helps identify a magical concept; otherwise omit it.

Composition: Square canvas. Center the complete subject and let it occupy
roughly 75 to 82 percent of the canvas. Use a compact, instantly recognizable
silhouette with generous, even padding on every side. Keep all important parts
inside the canvas. The icon must remain clear when printed approximately 25 mm
wide and when viewed at any rotation on a circular card.

Background: Genuine alpha transparency. Leave every pixel outside the symbol
transparent. Do not draw or bake a gray-and-white checkerboard, white matte,
colored backdrop, glow, cast shadow, floor, scenery, frame, circle, or badge.

Constraints: Exactly one main symbol. No unrelated props or duplicated parts.
No text, letters, numbers, logo, trademark, watermark, or signature. Avoid tiny
details, thin fragile lines, visual clutter, photorealism, complex textures,
heavy gradients, lens effects, and dark background elements. Use an original
public-domain-inspired design and do not imitate a modern film, animation
studio, game, or copyrighted character adaptation.

Consistency check before final output: Confirm a pure-black outer outline,
simple black interior lines, crisp faceted color areas, large white highlights,
one strong readable silhouette, square composition, even padding, and genuine
transparent background without a baked checkerboard.
